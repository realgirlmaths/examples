A set of worked examples showing how the breakeven calculator handles real purchasing decisions, from jewellery to footwear.

Example 1

Costume jewellery vs fine jewellery (designer brand)

A piece of costume jewellery, a bangle brass with gold plating, from a French family-owned brand costs £150. A similarly designed piece of fine jewellery, a bangle of 18k gold, from Dior costs £9000.

Input

Cheap item cost£150
Replaced every2 years
Lifespan of quality item15 years
Actual price of quality item£9,000
Return rate7%
Inflation rate2.5%
Annual appreciation10% (optimistic)
Retained value at resale60%

Output

Breakeven max price £6,192

Conclusion

Fine jewellery is overpriced

Example 3

Cheap loafers vs Loro Piana loafers

A pair of beige loafers lined with faux fur cost £50 when purchased in Tokyo on vacation. Loro Piana's Summer Charms Walk Loafers, suede lined with beaver fur, cost £1785. No resale value expected despite the high end brand; the shoes will probably eventually get worn out. Calculator does not have ability to factor in longer lifespan with repairs.

Input

Cheap item cost£50
Replaced every1 year
Lifespan of quality item20 years
Actual price of quality item£1,785
Return rate7%
Inflation rate2.5%
Annual appreciation0%
Retained value at resale0%

Output

Breakeven max price £641
Breakeven for 10 years £388
Breakeven for 5 years £215

Conclusion

Loro Piana is overpriced
